Registration period

How registration works

Each step unlocks the next. Your dashboard always shows what is done and what is waiting on someone else.

  1. Create your account

    Use the registration number on your admission letter. You will sign in with it later.

  2. Book an appointment

    Confirm that you are within 2 km of campus, then pick a date and time. You receive a pass with a QR code.

  3. Collect four clearances

    Finance, University Hospital, your faculty and Sports each review your file. They approve it, or send a note explaining what to fix.

  4. Attend your appointment

    Show your pass at the registry desk. The registrar inspects your original documents and records the result.

  5. Print your confirmation

    Once the registrar approves your registration, the confirmation slip appears in your dashboard.

What to bring to the registry

Document Requirement
Admission letter Original and one photocopy
Academic certificates and result slips Originals, for inspection

Questions students ask

I am more than 2 km from campus. Can I still book?

No. Booking is only open within 4 km of campus. If you cannot get closer before the registration period ends, write to the registry so an officer can arrange an alternative.

Can I change my appointment?

Yes, until you check in at the registry desk. Open the Appointment page in your dashboard, cancel the booking and choose another slot.

What if I arrive late?

You are still seen if you arrive within 20 minutes of your slot. After that, book a new time.

Who can see my records?

Each department sees only what it needs for its own clearance. Finance does not see medical details, and the hospital does not see payment records.

A department returned my clearance. What now?

Open the Clearances page. The officer's note explains what to fix. Once you have fixed it, the officer reviews your file again.
